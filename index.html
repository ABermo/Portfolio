About Me

I'm Adam, I'm a 19 year old Data Science and AI student studying at TU Dublin, Grangegorman.

I have large interests in Coding, Mathematics, Speedcubing, Chess and Scrabble (I meet every definition of a nerd).

In Primary School I was selected to be assessed for Centre for Talented Youth Ireland (CTYI) based on advanced maths abilities.
I was lucky enough to be accepted and got to take mini college courses every Saturday, these courses included:

  • Mind Games, Strategies, Code Breaking
  • Veterinary Science
  • Hogwarts 101
  • Law and Political Science
  • Web Development
  • App Design

I was very fortunate to have parents who supported me with these courses, but those last two in particular sparked my love for coding.

Once I got to Leaving Cert I got the chance to be one of the first in my school to take Computer Science as a Leaving Cert subject.
I jumped at the opportunity, putting it as #1 for my choice subject. Here I had my first proper code learning experience, with CTYI I learned how it worked and what it did, but not how to use it.
Here I got to learn all about Python, learning the syntax, functions, libraries and its endless possibilities.
